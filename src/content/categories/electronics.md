Consumer electronics is a category where the details on the box carry most of the value. The same pair of earbuds can be a strong buy or a problem depending on whether it is sealed, which region it was made for, which generation it belongs to, and whether its UPC matches the listing it is going to be sold under. Experienced electronics buyers check those points first and talk price second.

Wholesale electronics for independent retail and online selling is mostly accessories and small devices: phone cases, cables and chargers, headphones, speakers, batteries, computer peripherals and smart home products. These items are compact and move quickly, and they make up much of the category's everyday volume. Larger devices appear too, usually as opportunistic lots when a model is being replaced.

The category also carries shipping rules that surprise new buyers. Almost anything rechargeable contains a lithium battery, and lithium batteries are regulated as hazardous materials in transport. That affects how a mixed order is packed and which carrier can take it, and it matters again when an online seller sends inventory into a fulfillment center. Warranties add another layer, since coverage comes from the manufacturer and its terms vary.
