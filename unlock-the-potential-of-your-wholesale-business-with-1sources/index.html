In today’s rapidly changing market, wholesalers face many challenges, from keeping up with inventory demands to ensuring timely deliveries. The success of your business depends on your ability to find reliable partners who can simplify these complex processes and keep your operations running smoothly. At 1Sources, we specialize in providing solutions that streamline product sourcing, distribution, and inventory management.

Where wholesale operators lose margin

With so much competition and so little room for logistical or distribution error, it’s getting more challenging than ever to find reliable products in stock when you need them most. Margin rarely disappears in one place. It leaks — through vendor admin, through stock sitting too long, through a delivery that lands a week after the demand peak that justified it.

Most of that leakage traces back to fragmentation. Purchase orders sit with one set of vendors, stock data with another, and freight with a third. Each handoff is a place where accuracy drops and cost rises.

Our in-depth product knowledge and strong vendor relationships allow us to knock down barriers between manufacturers and wholesalers to get more profitable products into your pipeline.

Finding products without the sourcing overhead

Managing purchase orders from multiple vendors can be streamlined. Our sourcing team meticulously researches products based on demand, data, and industry trends, so you can find household names that your customers already love and trust — rather than gambling on unproven lines.

That research is what turns a catalog into a shortlist. Choose from a vast selection of over 100,000 products from some of the largest brands in the world, across categories including beauty, toys and games, food and grocery, and more.

Getting to the brands your customers already trust

Brand access is the difference between a catalog that sells and one that sits. Our extensive database features over 900 trusted brands — including OPI, Kraft, Nestle, L’Oreal and more — spanning grocery, beauty, household goods and beyond.

We partner with leading brands to deliver reliable distribution solutions to manufacturers. By enhancing the overall supply of products, we help reduce the procurement and carrying costs that wholesalers often face.

Holding the right stock, not more stock

Say goodbye to the challenges of sourcing products from multiple vendors. Our streamlined operations ensure that your procurement, inventory, and deliveries are efficient and cost-effective.

The lever most operators underuse is forecasting. With 1Sources, you can review, understand, and customize your inventory forecasting to meet the specific needs of your growing business. Transparency in numbers ensures you make informed decisions that positively impact your bottom line.
