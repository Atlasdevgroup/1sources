Wholesale grocery runs on dates. A case of crackers, a pallet of pasta sauce or a mixed load of candy is worth what the date code allows you to do with it. Buyers who do well in this category know their sell-through rate on every item and translate remaining shelf life directly into how much they can take and what they should pay.

1Sources focuses on shelf-stable, name-brand grocery: snacks, confectionery, pantry staples, canned and jarred goods, breakfast items and beverages. These products can be stored in an ordinary dry warehouse and shipped without refrigeration, which keeps them practical for independent grocers, convenience stores and online sellers alike. The emphasis is on the recognizable brands and staples that most shoppers put in the cart every week.

Grocery buyers tend to work in two modes. The regular mode is replenishment on items with steady demand, where consistency and reliable dating matter most. The second is opportunistic: a manufacturer overproduces a flavor, a retailer cancels a promotion, or a packaging change leaves previous stock behind. Those lots can be very good value, provided the dating and quantity fit your shop and you can sell them before the code runs out.

Every grocery quote from 1Sources should give you what you need to make that call: case pack, product description and, for short-coded lots, the date. Your account contact can also help you time heat-sensitive products such as chocolate so they do not sit on a hot truck, and combine grocery with household or personal care items on a single order. If your channel has specific requirements, such as a minimum remaining shelf life or U.S.-only labeling, tell us when you apply so the offers you receive match them.
