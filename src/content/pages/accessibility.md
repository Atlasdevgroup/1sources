We want everyone who buys from, sells to or researches 1Sources to be able to use this website, including people who use assistive technology.

## Our standard

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at Level AA. In practice that means:

- Semantic HTML with one main heading per page and a logical heading order
- Full keyboard access, including the navigation menus, with a visible focus indicator
- Text and interface colors that meet AA contrast requirements
- Form fields with visible labels, clear error messages, and an error summary that receives focus
- Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images, and decorative images hidden from screen readers
- Respect for the "reduce motion" setting in your operating system
- Pages that reflow at 400% zoom and on small screens without horizontal scrolling

## Known limitations

Some external content we link to, such as government forms, may not meet the same standard. If you find a page on this site that doesn't work for you, we want to know.
