  - title: Aerosols and chemicals as limited-quantity hazmat
    body: >-
      Aerosol air fresheners, bleach, oven cleaners and some laundry products

  - title: Weight and cube drive the landed cost
    body: >-
      Liquid detergent is heavy and paper towels are bulky, and both are
      inexpensive per unit. Freight can be a large share of what you pay, so the
      same unit price can produce very different margins depending on how full
      the pallet is and how far it travels. Compare quotes on a landed basis,
      not unit price alone.
  - title: Disinfectant registration
    body: >-
      Products that claim to kill germs are pesticides under federal law and
      carry an EPA registration number. Some states also require their own
      product registration before a disinfectant can be sold there. If you
      resell disinfectants, check that the products you buy may be sold in the
      states where you ship.
  - title: Leaks and crushed cartons

      claim depends on documentation made at the dock, including photos of the
      pallet before it is broken down.

Household products are bought by the pallet as often as by the case. Laundry detergent, paper towels, trash bags and all-purpose cleaners sit at the center of grocery, discount and hardware trade, and shoppers compare those prices closely. A store's household aisle is where its value image is won or lost, so buyers look for name-brand product at a cost that allows a competitive shelf price.

The category has a practical challenge the others do not. Much of what it sells is heavy, bulky or regulated for transport. A pallet of liquid detergent weighs far more than a pallet of cosmetics, and aerosol air fresheners or bleach need hazmat handling on the way to your door. The real cost of a household buy is the unit price plus freight, and experienced buyers compare offers on that combined number.

Buying patterns follow that math. Grocery and discount accounts replenish core laundry and paper items on a regular cycle and add promotional buys when a brand has excess stock or is changing a formula or bottle size. Online sellers often focus on multipacks and compact, concentrated items that are cheaper to ship to customers. Janitorial resellers look for commercial-size containers and cleaning tools.

1Sources quotes household goods by the case and helps you plan orders that ship efficiently. A mixed pallet that combines heavy laundry products with lighter personal care or paper goods can bring freight per unit down, and one account can cover all of it. Your account contact identifies regulated items on the quote and confirms how the order will ship, so there are no surprises when the truck arrives.
