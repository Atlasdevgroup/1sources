This Privacy Policy explains how 1Sources ("1Sources", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in connection with the website at 1sources.com (the "Site") and the wholesale accounts we provide to businesses.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you apply for an account, contact us, or email or call us, we collect what you provide. That can include your name, role, business email, phone number, company name, business address, website, business type, sales channels, product categories of interest, an estimated purchasing range, your resale-certificate status, how you heard about us, and the content of your message.

We do not ask for tax identification numbers, bank details or documents through the Site. If we need them to open an account, we request them directly during onboarding.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser and device type, referring page and pages viewed). If analytics are enabled, we record how pages and features are used — for example, which pages are viewed and whether a form was started or submitted — and campaign parameters (such as `utm_source`) from the link that brought you to the Site.

## How we use information

- To review account applications and decide whether we can supply your business
- To respond to enquiries and send you offers and quotes you have asked for
- To manage your account, orders, invoices and deliveries
- To understand how the Site is used and improve it
- To keep the Site secure and prevent fraud or misuse
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

- Service providers that process it on our behalf — for example website hosting, form handling, email, customer relationship management and accounting software — under confidentiality obligations
- Carriers and logistics providers, to the extent needed to deliver orders
- Professional advisers, where necessary
- Authorities or other parties where required by law or to protect our legal rights

## Cookies and analytics

The Site works without cookies. If we enable analytics (such as Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager), those tools may set cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings without affecting how the Site works.

## Retention

We keep application and enquiry records for as long as we have, or are considering, a business relationship with you, and afterwards for as long as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you offers, by contacting us at the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, and we will respond within the time that law requires.

## Children

The Site is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
